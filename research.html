Research Interests

My research interests lie at the intersection of urban spatial inequality, environmental justice, and urban planning, with a particular focus on children’s opportunities for outdoor activities. Drawing on my experience in child-friendly urban planning, I am interested in how public and residential outdoor spaces, housing conditions, and the built and natural environments shape unequal opportunities across neighborhoods and socioeconomic groups.

I use geospatial analysis and human-centered GeoAI with multisource urban data, including remote sensing and street-level imagery, to assess urban environmental conditions and investigate spatial disparities. I am particularly interested in how environmental exposures and unequal access to resources affect disadvantaged communities, and how these insights can inform more equitable urban planning and spatial resource allocation.
