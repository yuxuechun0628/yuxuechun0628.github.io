ABOUT / RESEARCH & PRACTICE

Urban research
& design.

I am an urban planner and researcher with a Master of Urban Design from The University of Hong Kong. I have over three years of professional planning experience, including work at the Shanghai Branch of the China Academy of Urban Planning and Design (CAUPD), where I contributed to child-friendly city initiatives and UNICEF-commissioned projects.

That experience led me to ask how urban environments shape children’s opportunities for outdoor activity and why these opportunities differ across communities. My research interests lie at the intersection of urban spatial inequality, environmental justice, and urban planning. In my current role as a Research Assistant at UT Austin, I work on multimodal urban sensing and GeoAI, developing methodological skills that I aim to combine with spatial analysis and community research to better understand children’s everyday environments.

I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.
