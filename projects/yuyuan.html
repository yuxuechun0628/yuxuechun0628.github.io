YUYUAN ROAD, SHANGHAI / 2025 / COLLABORATIVE STUDY

At a Child’s Pace

Making room to arrive, walk, play, and pause along the school route.

PROJECT CONTEXT

Yuyuan Road and Lane 1210, Shanghai 2025 Collaborative child-friendly streets study

FOCUS

School-route connections Street-edge adaptation Walking, waiting, and play

Everyday space beyond the gate

A school route connects cultural facilities, the children’s library, and neighborhood life. The study examines how narrow lanes, school entrances, and enclosed street edges can better support children and caregivers.

Three moments along the route

Arrival spaces create room to wait; walking corridors connect destinations; and seating, display, and interaction introduce shared uses along the street edge. The parking-relocation option remains conditional on relocation and traffic coordination.
