WAN CHAI, HONG KONG / GRADUATION DESIGN

Waterfront Loops

A connected public realm along Wan Chai North’s waterfront.

PROJECT CONTEXT

Wan Chai, Hong Kong Graduation design Tutor: Alain JF Chiaradia

FOCUS

Waterfront urban design Movement networks Public-space analysis

Reconnecting the waterfront

The design examines how the harbor edge can connect with inland streets, parks, and public destinations across transport infrastructure.

Cross-links, parallel routes, and loops

Three spatial alternatives test different connections between city and waterfront. The proposal combines elevated and ground-level routes with landscape, waterfront activities, and a sequence of public programs.
